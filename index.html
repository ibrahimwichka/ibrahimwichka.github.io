01.About me

I'm a student at Carnegie Mellon University studying Statistics & Machine Learning and Computer Science. I enjoy building machine learning systems that hold up in the real world, from research through to production.

I've worked across research labs and industry. Most recently, I've been a Robotics Research Intern at CMU's TRIO Lab and an ML Engineer Intern at Shop Scout.
